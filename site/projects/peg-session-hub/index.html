PEG Session Hub

Scheduling, game selection, and session planning in a dedicated FastAPI lane. The service owns its own schema, runs migrations during rollout, and stays behind a private-host deployment path.

Back to all projects Source

Service shape

  • API: FastAPI for browser or bot clients.
  • Data: PostgreSQL 16 with `session_hub` schema.
  • Rollout: GHCR image, compose startup, migrations, then health check.
